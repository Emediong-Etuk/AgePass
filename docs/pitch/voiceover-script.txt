RECORDING TIPS
--------------
- Record each cue as its own take; it makes lining them up in an editor easy.
- Keep the music bed (if any) very low, so the voice sits on top.
- The video has no audio track, so your voiceover is the only sound.
- To combine a finished voiceover.m4a with the video without re-encoding the picture:
    ffmpeg -i agepass-demo.mp4 -i voiceover.m4a -map 0:v -map 1:a -c:v copy -c:a aac agepass-demo-vo.mp4
