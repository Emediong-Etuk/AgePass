**2/**
Age-check laws are spreading across the UK, EU, Australia and US states.

Sites only need a yes or no. Today they collect full IDs, and every one of them becomes a
honeypot of identities.

**3/**
AgePass flips it:
1️⃣ Verify once with an issuer (bank, telco, KYC)
2️⃣ Your device proves "18+" with a zero-knowledge proof
3️⃣ The site learns: yes, plus a nickname that only works on that site
[06-approve-request.png]

**4/**
What never goes on-chain: your name, birth date, ID, or even which credential is yours.

Merkle-tree membership proofs on Midnight hide the leaf. Different sites get unlinkable
nicknames, so you can't be tracked across them.
[08-store-unlocked.png]

**5/**
Underage? The check fails on your own device before a proof even exists.
Nothing is shared. No transaction is sent.
[09-underage-blocked.png]

**6/**
Why @MidnightNtwrk: a public + private ledger, compiler-checked `disclose()` boundaries, and
shielded DUST fees. The privacy boundary lives in the contract, not in a promise.
